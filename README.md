# Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c).

## The fortune

> You will soon meet a wealthy stranger. He bought your liquidation.

11 words, 66 characters. One line, one breath.

## Why this angle wins

The field's strongest lines lean on lucky numbers, the stars, or a fortune
walking past the wallet. This one uses the single most canonical fortune
cookie opening there is — "You will soon meet a wealthy stranger" — which
no entry in the field touches, and breaks it with a turn nobody saw coming:
the stranger is not bringing wealth, he is the one who profited from your
loss. The loss and the trader are both in it, the joke is rueful rather
than cruel, and at 11 words there is nothing left to trim.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the fortune to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), fails
loudly if the line ever leaves the 1–160 character or 20-word gates, and
exits 0. No network, no randomness, no clock — the output is identical on
every run.
